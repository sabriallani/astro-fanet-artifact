## Manuscript blockers found

The current `/data/a3d-bsm-paper/main.tex` still contains older claims and
numbers that are not generated by the final validated candidate, including:

- GM3D results such as `97.8%`, `97.1%`, and `82.6%` in the main result and
  mobility tables;
- RPGM values such as `98.2%` and `85.3%`;
- a Byzantine figure caption claiming `97.1%` to `89.6%` degradation and a
  separately implemented `A3D-BSM-NT` comparison;
- claims of direct superiority over `SF`, `CB`, `PR`, `SBA`, and `3D-DNA-BSP`.

The public artifact audit explicitly states that the exact packet-level
implementations of those baselines are not all exposed and that a complete
end-to-end reproduction archive is not currently present.

## Required LaTeX correction order

1. Remove or replace stale numerical tables, captions, and claims that cannot be
   traced to a published CSV and manifest.
2. Add the validated bounded-two-hop progress-aware experiment as a conditional
   extension, not as a universal replacement for A3D-BSM.
3. State the exact valid-run counts, seeds, Byzantine fraction, confidence
   intervals, and the common RPGM failure.
4. Remove unsupported claims about exact third-party baseline superiority unless
   executable parity or frozen raw traces are added first.
5. Rebuild LaTeX and check fatal errors, undefined citations, undefined
   references, and remaining layout warnings.
6. Only after this alignment should the paper be judged ready for submission.

## Scope protection

This audit does not modify `main.tex`, `bib.bib`, the PDF, or the response
letter. It is a prerequisite record for the subsequent LaTeX correction pass.
